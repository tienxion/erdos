## Summary supplied by the user and submitted verbatim

We prove the conjectured size Ramsey formula for pairs of star forests whose diagonal maxima satisfy explicit local gap conditions. The proof builds on earlier simultaneous-tail lower bound by controlling the maximum-degree vertices in equality cases and applying Fournier’s edge-colouring theorem. This gives exact values for additional families, including families where it improves the earlier lower bound by arbitrarily many edges, but does not fully resolve this problem.
