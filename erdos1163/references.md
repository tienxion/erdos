# References and prior work

- Colva M. Roney-Dougal and Gareth Tracey, *Subgroups of symmetric groups:
  enumeration and asymptotic properties*, arXiv:2503.05416.
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05416),
  [version 1 text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05416v1).
  Theorem 2 supplies the upper bound for all 2-subgroups used in the
  submitted result. Theorem 1 and Proposition 7.3 provide relevant prior
  total-count estimates and lower-bound constructions.
- L. G. Kovács and Cheryl E. Praeger, *Finite permutation groups with large
  abelian quotients* (1989).
  [Author-hosted paper](https://archives.maths.anu.edu.au/people/Kovacs/K070.pdf).
  Section 2 describes the classical degree-eight group used in the construction.
- Garrett Birkhoff, *Subgroups of abelian groups* (1935).
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s2-38.1.385).
  Used in the broader all-abelian enumeration notes.
- Liam Naughton and Götz Pfeiffer, *Integer Sequences Realized by the Subgroup
  Pattern of the Symmetric Group*.
  [arXiv:1211.1911](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.1911).
  A source for finite subgroup-count comparisons in the research notes.
- [Erdős problem 1163](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1163) and the related
  [problem 1162](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1162).

The constructions use classical binary subspace counting. No claim is made
that these ingredients, the degree-eight group, or the published upper bound
are new. The literature check has not established the novelty of the precise
refinements here.
